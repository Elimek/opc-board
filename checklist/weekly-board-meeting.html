What not to do

Name the work you are dropping. An empty list means you have not chosen.

How a meeting counts

  • The owner decides. A row of high scores is not a yes.
  • A seat with no published SKILL does not vote.
  • A published SKILL owes a stance, at least one counter-option, evidence, and a confidence level.
  • Do not raise a number so that every seat looks like a 6 or higher.

A future skill is complete only when criticism materials are included, sealed test items stay out of the runtime package, and a fidelity check against a bare model has been run before it is marked complete.
