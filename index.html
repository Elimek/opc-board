⚙️ HOW IT WORKS

Three Modes, One System

三种模式，一个系统

1

Weekly Board Meeting

每周董事会

One decision. Zhang Xuefeng can take a stance. Empty seats stay blank. You decide. Open the checklist.

一个决定。张雪峰可以给立场。空席位留白。你做决定。打开清单。

2

Cash check

现金核对

Months of runway, money in, money out, and any offer you accepted or refused. No separate finance skill is published.

跑道还有几个月、进账、出账、有没有接或拒一份 offer。财务席位还没有 SKILL。

3

Five filters

五个滤镜

Compounding, health, content, first principles, and whether this gambles rent, survival, or a visa date. Then you still decide.

复利、健康、能不能变成内容、第一性原理、以及这是不是在赌房租、生存或签证日期。写完仍然由你决定。
